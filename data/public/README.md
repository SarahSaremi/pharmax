## Thresholds applied

- Ordinary drugs: **no small-cell suppression** (every cell is published).
- Sensitive drugs (lexicon flags `C`, `A`, `X`: controlled/psychotropic, obstetric/abortifacient-related, unlicensed sexual enhancers): cells with fewer than **5** distinct pooled senders are removed (3,041 cells). Totals for these drugs are therefore **under-counts**.

## Composition

- Roles among messages with text: supply 76.3%, demand 14.9%, ambiguous 2.9%, other 3.8%, job ads 2.1% (job ads are excluded from drug counts).
- **Media with no text: 3.5% of all messages** (mostly photos). Their content is invisible to this text-based analysis; photo posts that have a caption are analysed through the caption only.
- Messages from deleted accounts: 31.1% overall, from 65% in 2018 to 7% in 2026. All deleted accounts count as one sender, so distinct-sender counts are under-estimated, most in the years with the highest share.
- Share of extracted item lines that matched a lexicon drug group: **51.4%**. The rest are long-tail drugs, devices and noise.

## Lexicon flags

`C` controlled/narcotic/psychotropic/stimulant-type, `A` obstetric/abortifacient-related, `R` rare-disease/orphan-type, `V` COVID-era, `X` unlicensed sexual-enhancement. A flag marks the drug, not intent: e.g. misoprostol is also used for ulcers and postpartum bleeding.

## Known limitations

- Supply/demand labels come from rules tuned on **one** channel (informal spot check ~90-95%); accuracy on the others has not yet been measured. Treat role-level results as provisional until the QA described in [ADDING_A_CHANNEL](../../docs/ADDING_A_CHANNEL.md) is complete.
- The lexicon is unreviewed; brand-to-drug mapping was done by the project author.
- Sender linkage across channels uses display names, so two people with the same name merge and one person with several names splits.
- A mention is not a transaction, and a Telegram group is not the market. See [METHODOLOGY](../../docs/METHODOLOGY.md).

## Quick start

```python
import pandas as pd
cells = pd.read_csv('pooled_drug_month_counts.csv')
lex = pd.read_csv('lexicon.csv')[['drug_id', 'name', 'category', 'flags']]
demand = cells[cells.role == 'demand'].merge(lex, on='drug_id')
demand.groupby([demand.month.str[:4], 'name']).n_senders.sum().unstack(0).fillna(0)  # yearly sender-months
```
